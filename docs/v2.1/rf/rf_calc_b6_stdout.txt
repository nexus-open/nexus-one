--- 3. DETUNING RISK (ceramic patch behind 2.0 mm PC cap) ---
Satellite antenna per v2.0 design: 25x25x4 mm ceramic patch, RHCP,
3 dBi, tuned for the window. A high-eps ceramic substrate (eps~20-90)
dominates the stored field, so a thin PC superstrate (eps~2.9) pulls
f_res down only ~-1..-5%% (vs ~-10..-20%% for a low-eps patch).
RULE: antenna MUST be tuned with the 2.0 mm cap present (in-situ tune).
BUDGETED until measured: 0.5 dB untuned penalty, 0.2 dB tuned.
Axial-ratio degradation for RHCP: second order (<0.3 dB) with symmetric window.
(Full-wave confirmation needs HFSS/CST-class solver -> NOT-VERIFIED.)
