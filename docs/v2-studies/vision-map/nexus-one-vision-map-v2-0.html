Drum R8 with rack-and-pinion sync (m0.5, 32T); negator spring 12 N·mm; opening force ≈2.0 N; panel travel 30.9 mm. Left/right sequencing rules solved in v0.3–v0.7.
Magnetic lockMagnetic locking at open / closedPROVEN IN SIM
Slide friction is only 0.04 N, so the design rule is a magnetic lock ≥1.0 N per wing-equivalent, detents ≥3 N, sensed by DRV5032 switches. The poster's magnetic lock is load-bearing in the real design too.
Spine electronicsLayered internal architecture, smart connectorsPROVEN IN SIM
Tray board 140 × 26 mm fits the 4.5 mm tray (23.8% fill). ESP32-S3 controller, AS5600 drum encoder, BQ25890-class charger, MAX17048 gauge. Firmware compiles (572,989 B, 43% flash); logic model passes 12/12 tests. (Design catch found & fixed: encoder and gauge share I2C address 0x36 → separate buses.)
Rolling ribbon cableFlexible display roll connectionPROVEN IN SIM
FPC rolling U-loop at R6: tip travel 13.1 mm, take-up 15.7 mm, design target 54,750 cycles. A real FPC's rating at R6 dynamic is assumption-labelled, not datasheet-proven.
Internal batteryHigh-density modular battery layerPROVEN IN SIM
13.7 Wh (2 × 6.85 Wh — the same split your anatomy board shows). Stick idle 0.30 W → 45.7 h; camera mode 4.5 W → 3.0 h burst-duty.
Thermal systemVapor chamber / graphene cooling layerPROVEN IN SIM
The thermal law is proven, and it is a hard ceiling: ≤2 W folded, ≤1.5 W folded with battery module, ≤3 W open; Pocket Mode 0.9 W on hot days (43 °C skin limit). No vapor chamber inside the stick — the shell is the heat path. The poster's cooling layer maps to this law plus the cooling module.
Premium materialsTitanium alloy, carbon fiber, ceramic glassDESIGNED
v2.0 study: Ti-6Al-4V rails, CFRP back shell, ceramic accents, polymer caps STAY (RF windows — non-negotiable). Mass 241 → 261 g (+20 g, +8.3%). Titanium buys zero specific stiffness over aluminium (E/ρ 25.7 vs 25.5); the CFRP shell buys +71%. Conflict found: Ti conducts 25× worse than Al — the 2 W folded law does not survive the premium stack as drawn; graphite spreader + 1-D model required before any v2 freeze.
